# Pindah Private Limited

Pindah Private Limited is a premier enterprise software engineering and technology consulting firm based in Harare, Zimbabwe. We design, build, and deploy robust, scalable digital ecosystems and software-as-a-service (SaaS) platforms that empower institutions and commercial enterprises across the region to operate with world-class efficiency.

---

## 🚀 Our Core Product Ecosystem

Our corporate website serves as the central hub and portal for our proprietary enterprise solutions:

### 1. Frame — School Management Platform
Frame is a comprehensive, cloud-native administration and learning management solution designed specifically for primary and secondary (K12) schools in Zimbabwe. 
*   **Localized Architecture:** Engineered to align perfectly with regional grading structures, curriculum standards, and school fee tracking models.
*   **Seamless Communication:** Features automated billing, SMS/mobile alerts, and parent portals to bridge the gap between institutions and families.
*   **Scalable & Affordable:** Designed to reduce administrative overhead while ensuring high security for student and financial data.

### 2. Pindah ERP
A robust, all-in-one Enterprise Resource Planning application engineered to streamline commercial operations for mid-to-large-scale organizations.
*   **Supply Chain & Stock Control:** Real-time multi-branch inventory tracking, automated reorder thresholds, and point-of-sale (POS) integration.
*   **Financial Integrity:** Secure transaction logging, product lifecycle auditing, and comprehensive customer relationship management (CRM) workflows.

### 3. Custom Enterprise Implementations
Beyond our flagship platforms, Pindah engineers custom, high-performance software infrastructure for specialized operations, including:
*   **Pharmaceutical Logistics:** Specialized multi-branch dispensing and inventory architectures for large-scale pharmaceutical retail and distribution.
*   **Corporate Analytics:** Implementation of advanced organizational tools, including Balanced Scorecard tracking, psychometric data pipelines, and performance analytics.
