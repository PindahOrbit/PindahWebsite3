# Off-site SEO: Google Business Profile & Backlinks

On-site SEO (landing pages, titles, content, sitemap) is only half of local dominance in Zimbabwe. These steps must be done outside the codebase.

## Google Business Profile (GBP)

Create/claim a profile for **Pindah Private Limited** at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com).

| Field | Suggested value |
|-------|-----------------|
| Business name | Pindah Private Limited |
| Category | Software company (primary); also IT consultant / Computer software store if allowed |
| Address | Your Harare office address (or service-area business if you prefer) |
| Service area | Harare, Zimbabwe (and major cities you serve) |
| Phone | +263 714 856 897 |
| Website | https://pindah.org |
| Hours | Align with sales availability |

### GBP actions that move rankings

1. Verify the listing (postcard, phone, or email).
2. Add products/services: ERP Zimbabwe, School management software, Accounting software, Hospital systems, POS, Payroll.
3. Upload real photos: office, team, training sessions, product UI screenshots.
4. Post weekly updates linking to Insights articles (e.g. `/news/details/...`).
5. Ask satisfied clients for Google reviews (short, keyword-natural: “school fees software”, “accounting in Harare”).
6. Answer Q&A yourself: “Do you support ZiG?”, “Is Frame for private schools?”, “Do you integrate ZIMRA?”.

## Backlinks (Zimbabwe-focused)

Prioritize **relevant local and industry links** over raw volume.

### Quick wins

- [ ] List on Zimbabwe business directories (Yellow Pages ZW, local chambers, ICT association directories).
- [ ] Company page on LinkedIn with link to pindah.org; publish posts that link to SEO landings.
- [ ] Guest insight or listing with school associations, hospital networks, and chambers of commerce.
- [ ] Partner pages: hosting, fiscal device, or payment partners who will link to you.
- [ ] Tender profiles / supplier registries where software vendors are listed (with website URL).
- [ ] Case-study PDFs hosted on partners’ or clients’ sites (with permission) linking back.

### Content that earns links

- Publish comparison pages (`/sap-alternative-zimbabwe`, `/pastel-alternative-zimbabwe`) and promote them on LinkedIn.
- Offer a free one-page “ZIMRA software buyer checklist” PDF that cites `/zimra-compliant-accounting-software`.
- Sponsor or speak at Harare tech / education / healthcare forums and request a speaker bio link.

### Avoid

- Paid link farms and irrelevant foreign directories.
- Exact-match anchor spam (“best ERP Zimbabwe” on every link). Mix branded anchors (“Pindah”) with natural phrases.

## Measure

- Google Search Console: submit `https://pindah.org/sitemap.xml`, monitor impressions for Zimbabwe queries.
- GBP Insights: calls, direction requests, website clicks.
- Track rankings for: `school management software Zimbabwe`, `accounting software Zimbabwe`, `ERP Zimbabwe`, `SAP alternative Zimbabwe`.

2. Seeded Insights appear under `/news`.
3. `sitemap.xml` includes SEO slugs and `/news/details/{slug}` entries.
