# Forms & Surveys

## Executive Summary

Forms & Surveys lets you build, publish, and collect responses for questionnaires, checklists, registrations, and inspections — without separate survey tools. Design forms visually, share a link with respondents, and review submissions in one place.

---

## Who This Is For

- HR teams running onboarding or feedback surveys
- Operations teams collecting checklist data
- Marketing and events teams gathering registrations
- Quality teams running inspections

---

## Key Capabilities

- Drag-and-drop form builder
- Published public forms (shareable links)
- Response collection and review
- Form management and archiving

## Getting Started

Open **Forms** in the sidebar. **All Forms** lists every form in your organisation.

![Screenshot: Forms list — placeholder]

---

## Creating a Form

1. Go to **Forms → Create Form**.
2. Add a title and description.
3. Drag fields onto the canvas — text, choice, date, file upload, and more.
4. Mark required fields and set validation rules.
5. Save the form.

![Screenshot: Form builder — placeholder]

---

## Publishing a Form

When the form is ready:

1. Open the form and choose publish.
2. Copy the public link or embed instructions provided.
3. Share with respondents — no sign-in required for public forms unless you configure otherwise.

![Screenshot: Published form link — placeholder]

---

## Collecting Responses

Respondents open the link and complete the form in their browser. Submissions save automatically when they submit.

![Screenshot: Public form fill — placeholder]

---

## Reviewing Submissions

Return to **All Forms**, open a form, and view its responses. Export or filter responses for analysis.
