# Insurance

*Product guide — Operations Insurance Module*

## Executive Summary

Insurance is a full broker and insurer operating platform inside Operations — not a bolt-on policy register. It covers the complete lifecycle from **submission intake** through **quoting**, **placement**, **policy issue**, **endorsements**, **renewals**, **claims**, **reinsurance**, **regulatory reporting**, and **commission accounting**, with **seven inbuilt AI agents** that stream decision support from live portfolio data.

The **Insurance Command Center** gives executives and underwriters one screen for pipeline conversion, premium performance, loss ratio, open claims, outstanding premium, commission payable, and regulatory status. Brokers, underwriters, claims handlers, finance teams, and compliance officers work in a single connected workflow — with documents attached to every entity, configurable workflows, and optional self-service portals for brokers and policyholders.

Whether you run motor, property, commercial, travel, micro-insurance, or group schemes, the product catalogue is fully configurable. AI adapts to the product type at quote time — no separate analytics tool, no export to spreadsheets.

## Important Concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Submission** | New business intake — the starting point sent to the market for quoting |
| **Quote** | Terms, premium, and coverage proposed for a submission |
| **Placement** | Confirmation that an insurer bound the risk on agreed terms |
| **Policy** | Issued contract of insurance linked to a placement |
| **Endorsement** | Mid-term change to an active policy |
| **FNOL** | First Notice of Loss — initial claim registration |
| **Reserve** | Expected cost set aside for an open claim |
| **Client money** | Premiums held on behalf of insurers and clients |
| **Treaty** | Reinsurance arrangement ceding risk to reinsurers |
| **Loss ratio** | Incurred claims as a percentage of earned premium |

## Getting Started

1. Sign in to Operations.
2. Open **Insurance** in the sidebar (available when the Insurance module is activated for your organisation).
3. You land on the **Insurance Command Center** dashboard.

The sidebar groups the full insurance operation — submissions through reports — under one menu.

## Insurance Command Center

Open **Insurance → Dashboard**. The Command Center is your operational nerve centre for underwriting, premium performance, claims, reinsurance, and regulatory compliance.

![Screenshot: Insurance Command Center dashboard](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/02-insurance-dashboard.png)

### What you see

- **Underwriting pipeline** — submissions, quotes, and placements in the book
- **Active policies** — in-force count and policies due for renewal in the next 30 days
- **Open claims** — count and total paid to date
- **Loss ratio** — incurred versus earned premium with visual indicator
- **Premium metrics** — gross written premium, net written premium, earned premium, outstanding premium
- **Commission payable** — broker commission accruals
- **Reinsurance and regulatory** — contract count and filing status
- **Charts** — premium by product line, claims by status, monthly premium trend, loss ratio trend
- **Lifecycle chain** — visual flow from submissions through quotes, placements, and policies

### Quick actions

From the dashboard header you can jump to **Submissions**, **Quotes**, **Placements**, **Policies**, **Claims**, or **Reports**, create a **New submission**, open **Insights** (AI executive briefing), or **Refresh** data.

## AI Features

Insurance includes seven inbuilt AI decision points. Each streams analysis from live module data and supports follow-up questions in context — native to the ERP, not a separate chat tool.

| # | AI feature | Where to open it |
|---|------------|------------------|
| 1 | **Dashboard Insights** | Insurance → Dashboard → **Insights** |
| 2 | **AI Quote Builder** | Submissions list or submission detail → **AI Quote** |
| 3 | **AI Quote Comparison** | Insurance → Quotes → **Compare Quotes** |
| 4 | **AI Claims Triage** | Open a claim → **AI Triage** |
| 5 | **AI Analytics Reports** | Insurance → Reports |
| 6 | **AI Renewal Strategy** | Insurance → Policies → **Renewals** → **AI Renewal Strategy** |
| 7 | **AI Treaty Analysis** | Insurance → Reinsurance → **Contracts** → **AI Treaty Analysis** |

### Dashboard Insights

Click **Insights** on the Insurance Command Center. The AI reads your portfolio KPIs and produces an executive briefing — portfolio posture, key metrics, strategic insights, and priority actions. Ask follow-up questions in the panel; the conversation keeps dashboard context.

![Screenshot: Dashboard AI Insights panel](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/ai%20dashboard.PNG)

### AI Quote Builder

From **All Submissions** or a submission detail page, choose **AI Quote**. The AI reads the submission and risk details, then streams a structured quote draft — coverages, limits, deductibles, premium, rating basis, and terms. Edit the preview, refine with natural-language prompts, then **Save Quote** as a formal quote version.

![Screenshot: AI Quote Builder offcanvas](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/ai%20quote.PNG)

### AI Quote Comparison

Open **Insurance → Quotes → Compare Quotes** and select multiple quotes. The page builds a side-by-side AI comparison with premiums, terms, pros and cons, and a binding recommendation for underwriters.

### AI Claims Triage

Open a claim under **Insurance → Claims**, then click **AI Triage**. The AI assesses severity, liability, reserve adequacy, fraud indicators, and recommended next steps from the claim file, reserves, and payments.

### AI Renewal Strategy and Treaty Analysis

On **Renewals**, use **AI Renewal Strategy** for retention and repricing guidance. On **Reinsurance → Contracts**, use **AI Treaty Analysis** to review layer adequacy, cession efficiency, and exposure gaps.

## Submissions

A submission is the starting point for new business sent to insurers.

View all submissions under **Insurance → Submissions → All Submissions**. Use **AI Quote** from the row actions menu to generate a quote from any submission.

![Screenshot: All submissions list](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/03-all-submissions.png)

### Creating a submission

1. Go to **Insurance → Submissions → New Submission**.
2. Enter reference, status, product, effective and expiry dates, and risk description.
3. Attach supporting documents and add risk items.
4. Save and track status through the underwriting process.

![Screenshot: New submission form](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/04-new-submission.png)

## Quotes and Placements

### Quotes

1. Open **Insurance → Quotes → New Quote** (or create from a submission via **AI Quote**).
2. Enter terms, premium, and coverage details.
3. Save and send to the client or market.

Use **Compare Quotes** for AI-powered side-by-side comparison before binding.

### Placements

When a quote is accepted, record the **Placement** — confirming which insurer bound the risk and on what terms.

1. Go to **Insurance → Placements → New Placement**.
2. Link to the accepted quote and submission.
3. Confirm placement details and effective dates.

---

## Policies

### Issuing a policy

1. Open **Insurance → Policies → Issue Policy**.
2. Complete policy details from the placement.
3. Issue documents to the client.

### Endorsements

Record mid-term changes under **Endorsements** — coverage changes, cancellations, or corrections.

### Renewals

**Renewals** lists policies approaching expiry. Review, re-quote, and issue renewal policies from this queue. Use **AI Renewal Strategy** for retention and repricing guidance.

---

## Claims

### First notice of loss (FNOL)

1. Go to **Insurance → Claims → File FNOL**.
2. Select the policy, loss type, location, and incident details.
3. Submit to open a full claim record for ongoing handling.

### Reserves and payments

- **Reserves** — set and adjust expected claim costs by type
- **Payments** — record claim payments to beneficiaries or service providers

Open any claim and use **AI Triage** for severity, reserve adequacy, and fraud analysis. Attach survey reports, photos, and assessment forms via the built-in document panel on each claim.

## Reinsurance and Risk

### Reinsurance

Manage outward reinsurance under **Insurance → Reinsurance**:

- **Contracts** — treaty and facultative arrangements
- **Layers** — programme structure
- **Allocations** — how risk and premium cede to reinsurers

Use **AI Treaty Analysis** on the contracts list for programme review.

### Risk management

- **Risk Items** — insurable objects and exposures on submissions and policies
- **Risk Factors** — rating factors applied to pricing and underwriting

## Workflows, Products, and Portals

Configure automated steps under **Workflows** and business logic under **Rules** — for example routing submissions by line of business or escalating overdue tasks.
