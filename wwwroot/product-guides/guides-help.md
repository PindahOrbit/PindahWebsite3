# Guides & Help

## Executive Summary

Guides & Help provides built-in assistance inside Operations. Open the help panel from the header or footer to browse system guides, how-to articles, and contextual help without leaving your current task.

---

## Who This Is For

- New users learning the system
- Any user who needs a quick reminder of how to complete a task
- Trainers onboarding teams

---

## Key Capabilities

- In-app help panel
- Searchable guides
- Contextual assistance from any screen

## Opening Help

Click **Help** in the page header or footer. A side panel opens with the guides library.
