## 1. What you are buying (in plain English)

**Pindah Basa** is business software that connects stock, money, people, and industry work in **one login**.

Today many organisations run like this:

| Without Pindah Basa | With Pindah Basa |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Excel for stock, another app for invoices, WhatsApp for approvals | One menu, one product list, one set of customers |
| Re-typing the same order into warehouse and finance | Buy → receive → sell → pay updates automatically |
| “Who has the latest numbers?” | Live dashboards from the same data |
| Switching products as you grow | Turn modules **on** as you need them |

You only switch on what you need. A corner shop and a hospital share the same foundation — different menus, same platform.

## 3. Sample workflows (how a normal day looks)

### A. Buy → Stock → Sell → Pay (retail & wholesale)

The heartbeat of any trading business.

```
Staff request     Manager approves     Order to supplier
      │                  │                     │
      ▼                  ▼                     ▼
 Requisition  →   Approval  →   Purchase Order  →  Goods arrive
                                                      │
                                                      ▼
                                              Stock quantity ↑
                                                      │
                         ┌────────────────────────────┘
                         ▼
              Cashier sells (POS) or invoice credit customer
                         │
                         ▼
              Stock ↓ · Money recorded · Reports update
                         │
                         ▼
              Supplier invoice matched (3-way) → Pay supplier
```

**What the buyer cares about:** no double entry; stock and cash stay in sync; approvals leave a clear trail.

---

### B. Clinic / hospital visit (healthcare)

```
Reception books patient  →  Clinician consults & notes
         │                           │
         ▼                           ▼
   Appointments / wards        Labs, theatre (HMS)
         │                           │
         └───────────┬───────────────┘
                     ▼
              Pharmacy dispenses (batch + expiry)
                     │
                     ▼
         Stock ↓ · Bill / medical aid · Accounting
```

**Optional add-ons:** home delivery of medicines; insurance claims; HR for clinical staff.

---

### C. School term (education)

```
Enrol learner → Classes & subjects → Attendance & marks
                      │
                      ▼
               Report cards generated
                      │
        ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
        ▼             ▼             ▼
   Fee invoices    Teacher HR    Parent CRM
   (Accounting)    (Payroll)     (follow-ups)
        │
        ▼
   Optional: school buses (Logistics)
```

Built with **Zimbabwe primary/secondary** structures in mind (grades, forms, terms).

---

### D. Insurance broker deal

```
Client submission → Quote → Policy issued → Renewals
                           │
                           ▼
                    Claims & evidence (Documents)
                           │
                           ▼
              Premiums & commissions (Accounting)
                           │
                           ▼
              Ask Analyst: “What renews in 30 days?”
```

### E. Delivery / fleet day (logistics)

```
Client order → Assign driver & vehicle → Trip on map
                      │
                      ▼
              Proof of delivery → Invoice in Accounting
```

Pharmacy home delivery uses the same delivery thinking for medicines.

## 5. Buyer's guide — choose with confidence

### Step 1 — Name your pain (pick the top 2)

- [ ] Stock and sales numbers never match  
- [ ] Approvals and purchases live in email/WhatsApp  
- [ ] Invoices and expenses are late or incomplete  
- [ ] Industry work (patients, learners, policies, trips) sits outside finance  
- [ ] Too many passwords and duplicate customer lists  
- [ ] Owners cannot see a live picture of the business  

### Step 2 — Match pain to a starter pack

| If your pain is mainly… | Start with |
|-------------------------|------------|
| Buying and selling goods | **Procurement + Stock + Accounting** |
| Dispensing medicines | **Pharmacy + Stock + Accounting** |
| Seeing patients | **Clinic or HMS** (+ Pharmacy if you dispense) |
| Running a school | **Frame** (+ Accounting for fees) |
| Broking insurance | **Insurance + Accounting + Documents** |
| Moving goods/people | **Logistics + Accounting** (+ CRM) |
| Formulating feed | **Agriculture + Stock + Procurement** |

### Step 3 — Ask us (or yourself) these buyer questions

1. **How many people will sign in?** (cashiers, teachers, nurses, managers)  
2. **Do we sell over the counter, on credit, or both?**  
3. **Do we need industry screens on day one, or only after stock and money work?**  
4. **Who approves purchases and who sees financial reports?**  
5. **Cloud now, or do we need on-premise / hybrid later?**  
6. **Which reports must the owner see every Monday morning?**  

### Step 4 — What “good” looks like in 30 days

| Week | Focus |
|------|--------|
| **1** | Organisation set up · users & roles · modules on · company branding |
| **2** | Products, warehouses, chart of accounts, payment methods |
| **3** | First live workflow (POS, receive stock, or enrol learners / book patients) |
| **4** | First clean report (P&L, stock on hand, or school/clinic dashboard) |

### Step 5 — How to buy / next action

1. Visit **[basa.pindah.org](https://basa.pindah.org)** and explore the live platform.  
2. Email **[admin@pindah.org](mailto:admin@pindah.org)** with your industry and starter pack from the table above.  
3. Book a short demo focused on **your** workflow (not every module).  
4. Go live with a small team; expand modules as confidence grows.
