# Healthcare (Clinic)

## Executive Summary

Healthcare — shown as **Clinic** in the menu — supports clinical facilities from small practices to hospitals and surgery centres. Manage appointments, patient records, clinical notes, laboratory orders, billing, teleconsultation, inpatient care, and operating rooms according to your facility type.

---

## Who This Is For

- Doctors, nurses, and clinical administrators
- Reception and scheduling staff
- Laboratory technicians
- Billing and insurance coordinators
- Hospital ward and theatre teams

---

## Key Capabilities

- Appointments and waitlists
- Patient registration and records
- Electronic medical records (EMR)
- Laboratory orders and sample tracking
- Insurance claims
- Teleconsultation
- Doctor schedules and shift rosters
- Consent forms
- Inpatient admissions, wards, and beds
- Operating rooms
- Clinic reports and dashboard

## Getting Started

Open **Clinic** in the sidebar. The **Clinic Dashboard** summarises today’s appointments, admissions, and open lab orders.

![Screenshot: Clinic dashboard — placeholder]

Your organisation’s facility type (clinic, hospital, surgery centre, dental practice, or multi-specialty) determines which menu items appear.

---

## Appointments

### Booking an appointment

1. Go to **Clinic → Appointments → Appointment List**.
2. Create a new appointment with patient, provider, date, and reason.
3. Confirm and notify the patient as per your process.

![Screenshot: Appointment list — placeholder]

### Waitlist

When slots are full, add patients to the **Waitlist**. Move them to a confirmed appointment when space opens.

![Screenshot: Appointment waitlist — placeholder]

---

## Patients

Register and search patients under **Clinic → Patients**. Each patient record links to appointments, EMR encounters, lab orders, and billing.

![Screenshot: Patient registration — placeholder]

---

## Electronic Medical Records

### Encounters

Document each visit under **EMR → Encounters**. Record history, examination findings, diagnosis, and plan.

![Screenshot: Clinical encounter — placeholder]

### Templates

Use **EMR → Templates** for standard note structures — speeding documentation while keeping consistency.

### Medical history

View longitudinal history under **EMR → Medical History**.

![Screenshot: Medical history — placeholder]

---

## Laboratory

### Ordering tests

1. Open **Lab → Lab Orders** from a patient or encounter.
2. Select tests from the **Test Catalog**.
3. Submit the order and print collection labels if needed.

### Sample tracking

Track specimens through collection, processing, and result entry under **Sample Tracking**.

![Screenshot: Lab order and sample tracking — placeholder]

---

## Billing and Insurance Claims

Raise patient bills and submit **Insurance Claims** where applicable. Claims link to encounters and insurer requirements.

![Screenshot: Insurance claims — placeholder]

---

## Teleconsultation

Run virtual consultations from **Teleconsult → Virtual Lobby**. Patients join from their invitation link; clinicians conduct the session from the lobby screen.

![Screenshot: Virtual lobby — placeholder]

---

## Scheduling

- **Doctor Schedules** — define when each clinician is available
- **Shift Roster** — plan ward and department shifts
- **Consent Forms** — manage templates and signed consents

![Screenshot: Doctor schedule — placeholder]

---

## Inpatient Care

Available for hospital-type facilities:

1. **Admissions** — admit a patient with ward and bed assignment
2. **Wards & Beds** — manage capacity and occupancy

## Surgery and Operating Rooms

Surgery centres and hospitals can manage **Operating Rooms** — booking theatres and linking to surgical encounters.
