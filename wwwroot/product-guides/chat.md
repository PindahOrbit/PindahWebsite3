# Chat

## Executive Summary

Chat provides in-app messaging so teams can communicate without leaving Operations. Open conversations from the profile menu and exchange messages with colleagues in real time.

---

## Who This Is For

- All staff who need quick internal communication
- Teams coordinating on orders, claims, projects, or daily operations

---

## Key Capabilities

- Direct and group conversations
- Real-time message delivery
- Accessible from the profile menu

## Starting a Conversation

1. Open Chat.
2. Start a new conversation or select an existing one.
3. Choose the colleague or group to message.

## Sending Messages
