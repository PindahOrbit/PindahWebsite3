# Dashboard

## Executive Summary

The Dashboard is your home screen after sign-in. It brings together summaries and shortcuts from the modules your organisation uses — finance, inventory, HR, school, CRM, logistics, insurance, and more — so you can see what matters without opening every area separately.

---

## Who This Is For

- Executives and managers who need a quick overview
- Department heads monitoring their area
- Any user who wants a starting point for the day

---

## Key Capabilities

- Module-specific summary views
- Quick links into detailed screens
- At-a-glance indicators for active areas of the business

## Opening the Dashboard

After sign-in you are taken to the dashboard automatically. You can return at any time by clicking **Home** in the breadcrumbs or choosing **Dashboard** in the sidebar.

## Understanding Dashboard Views

Depending on which modules are active, you may see dedicated dashboard sections such as:

| Area | What you typically see |
|------|------------------------|
| Financial | Sales, receivables, and accounting highlights |
| Inventory | Stock levels and movement summaries |
| HR & Payroll | Headcount and payroll-related indicators |
| School | Student and assessment overview |
| CRM | Pipeline and activity summaries |
| Logistics | Trips and fleet activity |
| Insurance | Submissions, policies, and claims overview |
| Agriculture | Feed and nutrient planning summaries |
| Projects | Project and job card status |

## Using Dashboard Shortcuts

Most dashboard tiles and figures link directly to the detailed list or report behind them. Click a figure or **View all** style link to drill down.

### Typical workflow

1. Open the dashboard at the start of the day.
2. Scan sections relevant to your role.
3. Click through to any item that needs action.
4. Return to the dashboard using **Home** in the breadcrumbs.

---

## Customising What You See

The sections shown depend on:

- Which modules your organisation has activated
- Which permissions your role includes

If a section is missing, ask your administrator whether the module is enabled and whether your role includes access to it.
