# Projects

## Executive Summary

Projects helps teams plan work, track progress, and manage job cards for field and workshop operations. See all active projects at a glance, drill into tasks and issues, and use job cards for structured maintenance or service work.

---

## Who This Is For

- Project managers and team leads
- Field service and maintenance teams
- Operations managers tracking delivery of work

---

## Key Capabilities

- Project dashboard
- Project list and detail
- Issues and task tracking
- Job cards and job card items

## Creating and Managing Projects

### Creating a project

1. From **All Projects**, create a new project.
2. Enter name, description, dates, and responsible owner.
3. Add team members and milestones.
4. Track progress as work completes.

### Updating status

Move projects through your organisation’s statuses — for example Planned, In Progress, On Hold, and Complete.

![Screenshot: Create project — placeholder]

---

## Job Cards

Job cards structure repeatable service or maintenance work.

1. Go to **Projects → Job Cards**.
2. Create a job card with customer, location, and work description.
3. Add line items — parts, labour, and tasks.
4. Assign to a technician and track completion.

![Screenshot: Job card — placeholder]

## Issues and Tasks

Track individual tasks and blockers as **issues** within a project. Assign owners, set priority, and close when resolved.
