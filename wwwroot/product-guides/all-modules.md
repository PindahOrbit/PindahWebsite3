# Pindah Basa — All Modules

## What is Pindah Basa?

**Pindah Basa** is business software that helps you run your company from one place. Instead of using separate apps for stock, money, staff, patients, or school records — and copying the same information into each one — **Pindah Basa keeps everything connected**.

You sign in once. You see one menu on the left. You turn on only the parts your business needs. When someone sells a product, receives goods from a supplier, or pays an invoice, the rest of the system updates automatically.

**Pindah Basa** is built by **Pindah Private Limited**. Try the platform at [basa.pindah.org](https://basa.pindah.org), or learn more about the company at [pindah.org](https://pindah.org).

## How the platform is organised

Think of Pindah Basa in four simple groups:

### 1. The basics (every organisation uses these)

These are the parts you need before anything else works:

- **[Core Platform](https://basa.pindah.org)** — The main screen, menu, and look of the system for your company.
- **[Authentication & Security](https://basa.pindah.org)** — How people sign in, reset passwords, and keep accounts safe.
- **[Organisation](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Where the owner or IT person adds users, chooses which modules are on, and sets company details.
- **[Dashboard](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Your home page after sign-in — summaries and shortcuts for the areas you use.
- **[Account Settings](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Each person’s own profile, password, and display preferences.

### 2. The core business trio (shops, warehouses, and distributors)

Most trading businesses rely on three modules that work together:

- **[Inventory & Stock](https://basa.pindah.org)** — What you sell and store: products, quantities, warehouses, suppliers, receiving goods, stock counts.
- **[Accounting & Sales](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Money: till sales, customer invoices, expenses, bank records, profit-and-loss reports.
- **[Procurement](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Buying from suppliers: staff requests, approvals, orders, matching invoices to what was received, and paying suppliers.

### 3. Industry-specific modules (add when you need them)

Hospitals, schools, insurance brokers, and feed manufacturers do not use the same screens as a corner shop. Pindah Basa offers specialised modules that still share the same products, customers, and money records. These are described in detail below.

### 4. Tools that help everyone

- **[Documents](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Store and share files (contracts, policies, attachments).
- **[Forms & Surveys](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Build forms and collect answers online.
- **[Chat](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Message colleagues inside the app.
- **[Mobile Scanner](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Use a phone to scan barcodes for stock work.
- **[Analyst](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Ask questions about your business data in plain language.
- **[Guides & Help](https://basa.pindah.org)** — Help topics inside the product.

## What is shared across modules?

When information is entered once, other areas can use it. That is the main benefit of one platform.

- **Products** — The same item list is used for stock, sales, pharmacy, purchasing, and reports.
- **Customers and patients** — A person you bill in accounting can be the same patient in clinic or parent in CRM.
- **Suppliers** — Vendor details used when ordering and when receiving stock.
- **Staff (users)** — Employee logins and permissions controlled in one place.
- **Money records** — Sales, supplier bills, school fees, and insurance premiums can all flow into your financial reports.

You do not maintain five different product lists or re-type the same invoice in warehouse and finance systems.

## The core trio explained (buy, stock, sell)

Here is how the three main business modules fit together in everyday language.

### Step 1 — Someone needs to buy something ([Procurement](https://basa.pindah.org))

  - A staff member fills in a **purchase requisition** (an internal request: “we need 50 reams of paper”).
  - A manager **approves** or rejects it.
  - Procurement turns the request into a **purchase order** sent to the supplier.
  - Optionally, several suppliers can quote first (RFQ) before you choose one.

### Step 2 — Goods arrive ([Inventory & Stock](https://basa.pindah.org))

  - Warehouse staff **receive stock** — they record what actually arrived, where it was put, and at what cost.
  - Quantities on the system go up.
  - Product costs can update for pricing.

### Step 3 — You sell or bill ([Accounting & Sales](https://basa.pindah.org))

  - **Point of Sale** — cashier sells over the counter; stock goes down; money is recorded.
  - **Invoices** — you bill credit customers; payments are tracked.
  - **Reports** — trial balance, profit and loss, and other finance views.

### Step 4 — You pay the supplier ([Procurement](https://basa.pindah.org) + Accounting)

  - You record the **supplier’s invoice**.
  - The system checks it against the order and what you received (**3-way match**).
  - After approval, you record **payment** to the supplier.

Purchase orders live under Accounting; receiving lives under Stock; requests and supplier bills live under Procurement — but they are one connected process, not three separate spreadsheets.

## Industry modules explained

### People and sales

**[HR & Payroll](https://basa.pindah.org)**

  - Employee records, job details, and contact information.
  - Running payroll and payslips.
  - Onboarding new staff and offboarding leavers.
  - Performance reviews and internal announcements.
  - Works with school (teachers) and clinic (clinical staff) when those modules are on.

**[CRM](https://basa.pindah.org)** (Customer Relationship Management)

  - Track **leads** and **opportunities** through your sales pipeline.
  - Store **accounts**, **contacts**, and customer history.
  - Log activities, support **cases**, and send **quotes**.
  - Connects to accounting customers and school admissions follow-up.

### Healthcare and pharmacy

**[Healthcare (Clinic)](https://basa.pindah.org)**

  - Appointments, patient registration, clinical notes.
  - Laboratory orders, billing, and insurance claims.
  - Suitable for clinics and outpatient facilities.

**[Hospital / HMS](https://basa.pindah.org)** (Hospital Management System)

  - Everything in Clinic, plus wards, beds, operating theatre, and fuller hospital workflows.
  - One system for reception through to billing — not separate patient, lab, and pharmacy databases.

**[Pharmacy](https://basa.pindah.org)**

  - Dispense medicines to patients with batch and expiry tracking.
  - Print labels, manage refills, and optional payment at the till.
  - Uses the same medicine stock as Inventory; patients can come from Clinic.

**[Pharmacy Home Delivery](https://basa.pindah.org)**

  - Plan and track delivery of medicines to patients’ homes.
  - Builds on the Pharmacy module.

### Education

**[School (Frame)](https://basa.pindah.org)**

  - Learners, classes, subjects, marks, and report cards.
  - Attendance and online assessments.
  - Built for Zimbabwe primary and secondary school structures (grades, forms, terms).
  - Fees can be handled in Accounting; teachers in HR; parent communication in CRM.

### Insurance

**[Insurance](https://basa.pindah.org)**

  - Full workflow for brokers and insurers: submissions, quotes, policies, renewals, claims.
  - Commission and regulatory reporting.
  - AI-assisted insights on the portfolio.
  - Links to accounting for premiums and to Documents for policy files.

### Operations and field work

**[Logistics](https://basa.pindah.org)**

  - Manage drivers, vehicles, clients, and trips.
  - See active deliveries on a map (command centre).
  - Useful for delivery companies, school transport, and distribution.

**[Projects](https://basa.pindah.org)**

  - Plan projects, track tasks and issues.
  - **Job cards** for workshop or field service work.

**[Asset Management](https://basa.pindah.org)**

  - Register laptops, vehicles, furniture, and equipment.
  - Record who has what and where it is.

**[Agriculture & Feed](https://basa.pindah.org)**

  - Design animal feed formulas from nutrients and raw materials.
  - Raw materials tie into Inventory; purchasing ties into Procurement.

## Real examples: how modules connect

### A retail shop or wholesaler

  1. **Procurement** — Manager approves a request; purchase order goes to supplier.
  2. **Stock** — Goods arrive; quantities updated in the warehouse.
  3. **Accounting** — Cashier sells on POS; stock reduces; sale appears in reports.
  4. **Procurement** — Supplier invoice matched and paid.

### A pharmacy

  1. **Clinic** (optional) — Doctor sees patient and prescribes.
  2. **Pharmacy** — Pharmacist dispenses; oldest batch used first; label printed.
  3. **Stock** — Medicine quantity drops automatically.
  4. **Accounting** — Payment recorded if the patient pays at the counter.

### A school

  1. **Frame** — Teachers enter marks; report cards generated.
  2. **Accounting** — Fees and tuck-shop sales billed and collected.
  3. **HR** — Teacher records and payroll.
  4. **CRM** — Follow up with parents on admissions or enquiries.
  5. **Logistics** (optional) — Bus routes and trips.

### An insurance broker

  1. **Insurance** — Client submission quoted and converted to policy.
  2. **Accounting** — Premiums and commissions in the books.
  3. **Documents** — Policy documents and claim evidence stored.
  4. **Analyst** — Ask questions about claims trends or portfolio performance.

## What should my organisation start with?

You do not need every module on day one. Common starting points:

**Retailer or distributor**

  - Start: Stock, Accounting, Procurement
  - Add later: CRM (more customers), HR (staff), Mobile Scanner (faster stock work)

**Pharmacy**

  - Start: Stock, Accounting, Pharmacy
  - Add later: Clinic (prescriptions), Delivery, Procurement

**Hospital or clinic**

  - Start: Clinic or HMS, Pharmacy, Stock, Accounting
  - Add later: Insurance (medical aid claims), HR

**School**

  - Start: Frame (school module), Organisation (users)
  - Add later: HR, Accounting (fees), CRM (parents), Logistics (buses)

**Insurance broker**

  - Start: Insurance, Accounting, Documents
  - Add later: CRM, Analyst

**Feed manufacturer**

  - Start: Agriculture, Stock, Procurement
  - Add later: Accounting, Projects

**Transport / logistics company**

  - Start: Logistics, CRM, Accounting
  - Add later: Stock, HR

Your administrator can turn modules on or off as the business grows — without changing to a different product.
