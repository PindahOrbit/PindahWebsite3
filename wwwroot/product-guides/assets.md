# Asset Management

## Executive Summary

Asset Management tracks organisational assets — equipment, devices, furniture, and other valuable items. Record what you own, who has it, where it is, and its condition over time.

---

## Who This Is For

- IT and facilities teams
- Finance teams tracking capital items
- Department managers accountable for equipment

---

## Key Capabilities

- Asset register
- Assignment to users or locations
- Asset history and status tracking

## Getting Started

Open **Assets** in the sidebar to view your organisation’s asset register.

## Asset Register

The register lists every tracked asset with tag number, category, location, assigned user, and status.

Use search and filters to find items due for return, maintenance, or audit.

![Screenshot: Asset register filters — placeholder]

---

## Adding Assets

1. Create a new asset from the register.
2. Enter description, serial number, purchase date, and value.
3. Set category and default location.
4. Save to add it to the register.

![Screenshot: Create asset — placeholder]

---

## Assigning Assets

Assign assets to employees or departments when issued. Record return dates when equipment comes back.

![Screenshot: Asset assignment — placeholder]

---

## Tracking and Reporting

Review asset history — assignments, transfers, and status changes — from each asset’s detail page. Export or filter the register for periodic audits.
