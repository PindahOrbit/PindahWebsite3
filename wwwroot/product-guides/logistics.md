# Logistics

## Executive Summary

Logistics manages fleet operations, drivers, clients, and trips — from planning routes to monitoring deliveries in real time. Use the command centre to see active trips on the map and keep customers informed about delivery progress.

---

## Who This Is For

- Logistics coordinators and dispatchers
- Drivers and field agents
- Fleet managers
- Customer service teams tracking deliveries

---

## Key Capabilities

- Logistics dashboard
- Drivers and vehicles
- Clients
- Trips and trip stops
- Command centre (live trip monitoring)
- Driver location tracking

## Getting Started

Open **Logistics** in the sidebar. The **Dashboard** shows active trips, available drivers, and fleet utilisation.

![Screenshot: Logistics dashboard](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/Screenshot%20(47).png)

---

## Drivers and Vehicles

### Drivers

1. Go to **Logistics → Drivers**.
2. Add drivers with contact details and licence information.
3. Assign drivers to vehicles and trips.

![Screenshot: Drivers list](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/Screenshot%20(48).png)

### Vehicles

Maintain your fleet under **Logistics → Vehicles** — registration, capacity, and maintenance notes.

![Screenshot: Vehicles list](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/Screenshot%20(49).png)

---

## Clients

Record delivery clients under **Logistics → Clients**. Clients link to trips as pickup or drop-off parties.

![Screenshot: Logistics clients](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/Screenshot%20(50).png)

---

## Planning Trips

### Creating a trip

1. Open **Logistics → Trips**.
2. Create a new trip with driver, vehicle, and route stops.
3. Add clients, addresses, and scheduled times for each stop.
4. Dispatch the trip when ready.

![Screenshot: Trip planning](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/Screenshot%20(51).png)

### Trip stops

Each trip consists of ordered **stops**. Update stop status as the driver arrives, loads, and completes delivery.

![Screenshot: Trip stops — placeholder]

---

## Command Centre

The **Command Centre** provides a live operational view of all active trips — ideal for dispatch desks.

1. Go to **Logistics → Command Center**.
2. View trips on the map and in the status list.
3. Identify delays and reassign resources if needed.

![Screenshot: Command centre map](https://storage.pindah.org/IMAGES/screenshots/Screenshot%20(52).png)

---

## Trip Monitoring

Track driver progress through GPS updates where enabled. Open an active trip to see current location, completed stops, and estimated arrival.
