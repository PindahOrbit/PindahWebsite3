# Organisation

## Executive Summary

Organisation settings control how your company appears and operates in the system — profile and branding, active modules, users, roles, billing, accounting periods, email templates, and security policies. Administrators spend most setup time here before rolling the system out to staff.

---

## Who This Is For

- Organisation owners and system administrators
- IT managers configuring access and security
- Finance leads setting periods and billing

---

## Key Capabilities

- General organisation profile
- Module activation
- Users and roles
- Apps and integrations profile
- Sales and inventory defaults
- Document settings
- Security settings
- Billing and subscription
- Accounting periods
- Email templates

## Getting Started

Open **Organisation** in the sidebar, or click your organisation name in the header. Settings are grouped into tabs for easy navigation.

![Screenshot: Organisation settings overview — placeholder]

---

## General Settings

Under **General**, set:

- Organisation name and legal details
- Logo and branding
- Contact information
- Regional preferences

![Screenshot: Organisation general tab — placeholder]

---

## Modules

Under **Modules**, choose which business areas are active — Stock, Accounting, CRM, Insurance, and others. Only activated modules appear in the sidebar for your users.

1. Review available modules.
2. Turn modules on or off according to your subscription and needs.
3. Save changes — users may need to refresh permissions.

![Screenshot: Organisation modules — placeholder]

---

## Users and Roles

### Users

Go to **Organisation → Users** to add staff, send invitations, and deactivate leavers.

![Screenshot: Organisation users — placeholder]

### Roles

Go to **Organisation → Roles** to define roles and assign permissions. Combine roles with user groups in HR for fine-grained access.

![Screenshot: Role management — placeholder]
