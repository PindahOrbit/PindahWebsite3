# Account Settings

## Executive Summary

Account Settings is your personal area within Operations. Update your profile, change your password, adjust display preferences, and manage your own security options — separate from organisation-wide settings managed by administrators.

---

## Who This Is For

- Every signed-in user managing their own profile

---

## Key Capabilities

- Profile details and avatar
- Password change
- Theme and display preferences
- Personal security settings

## Opening Account Settings

Click your name or avatar in the top-right corner of any page and choose **Settings**. You can also navigate directly to **Settings** in the sidebar under the Settings group.

![Screenshot: Profile menu — placeholder]

---

## Updating Your Profile

1. Open **Account Settings**.
2. Update your name, contact details, and profile photo.
3. Save changes.

Your profile photo appears in the header and on shared screens where your name is shown.

![Screenshot: Profile settings — placeholder]

---

## Changing Your Password

1. Open the security or password section of Account Settings.
2. Enter your current password and your new password twice.
3. Save — you may need to sign in again on other devices.

![Screenshot: Change password — placeholder]

---

## Display Preferences

Adjust theme (light or dark) and other personal display options where available. Preferences apply only to your account on the current browser.

![Screenshot: Theme settings — placeholder]

---

## Security Options

Manage personal two-factor authentication and connected devices if your organisation allows user-level control. Some security policies are set at organisation level — see **Organisation → Security** for organisation-wide rules.
