About Us

Alliance Health is a registered administrator of medical aid societies and schemes governed by the laws of the Republic of Zimbabwe. Alliance Health is a member of the Nzou Holdings group of companies with vast experience in the insurance sector including brokerage services and reinsurance. We strive to establish new levels of excellence in providing effective, innovative healthcare solutions and customer service to all our clients and members of Multimed, Alliance Health Options, Northern Alliance, Alliance Options Select and Northern Medical Aid Society.

We manage these solutions in conjunction with selected regional health care service partners.

Our Mission

To provide professional, efficient and personal healthcare service to our clients whilst remaining sensitive to the needs of all our stakeholders

Our Vision
